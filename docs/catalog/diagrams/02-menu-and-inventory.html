Service catalog · 02 of 05 · Two catalogs

The menu and the inventory

"Service catalog" means two different things. One is a list of what exists. The other is a list of what you're allowed to ask for. A platform needs both, and it works best when the first is generated from the second.

The inventory

Ownership, docs and dependencies for everything that runs. Useful, and famously hard to keep true when people maintain it by hand.

The menu

Typed APIs with a schema, defaults, outputs and an honest status. This is the part that defines the platform, and the part I built first.

Generated, not typed

In Hangar, Backstage's entities and its templates are both generated from Airframe's live XRDs and XRs, so the inventory stays true to what the menu created.
