Service catalog · 01 of 05 · The front door

One API, three kinds of caller

A developer at a portal, a pipeline making a commit, and an agent acting on someone's behalf all ask for the same things. If they each had their own way in, you'd have three platforms to keep honest.

Built

  • Fifteen APIs in Airframe, reconciled by Crossplane.
  • Backstage holds no Kubernetes credentials; its templates make git commits.
  • A generated contract bundle and llms.txt, checked in CI.

Proposed

Autopilot's runtime: agents reach the same APIs through Clearance, with narrow, audited authority. The Clearance core is built and tested; the real backends are not.

Why one door

Policy, audit and naming live in one place. A new kind of caller, even one we haven't thought of yet, inherits all of it for free.
