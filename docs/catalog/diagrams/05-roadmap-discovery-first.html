Service catalog · 05 of 05 · The approach

How I'd build a company's service catalog

A proposal, not a record: this is the plan I'd bring to a platform team on day one. The weeks overlap on purpose, and the numbers are starting points, not promises.

Discovery is the product work

Before any YAML: who are our developers, what do they wait on, and what do they copy from the last project? The answers become the first APIs.

Small, then readable, then open

One API that one team loves beats fifteen nobody asked for. Make it explain itself before you let an agent near it.

Measure people, not YAML

Take a baseline during discovery, then count time to first deploy and which APIs teams actually use. An API nobody calls is a cost, and retiring it is part of the job.
