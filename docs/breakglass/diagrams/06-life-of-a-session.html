The life of a session, and how it ends

Every clock in break-glass is short on purpose. Approval lapses, sessions expire, idle terminals end, and the cluster has its own timer in case everything else forgets.

Short by default

Pick the shortest duration that will do. One free extension, audited, and then it is a new request.

Clean up while allowed

The pod is restarted and the copy deleted while the grant still exists, and only then is the grant removed. Anything left over is written on the record as a note.

A backstop with no brain

The TTL label is set to the session plus fifteen minutes at creation. The cluster deletes the grant at that time, whatever state Backstage is in.
