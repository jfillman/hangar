Seven layers, each assuming the last one failed

Layers 1 to 3 are the portal being careful. Layers 4 to 6 are the cluster refusing to take the portal's word for it. Layer 7 is what makes the rest provable afterwards.

The portal

Permissions and backend rules stop the honest mistakes and the crafted requests: the wrong app, the wrong tier, your own approval.

The cluster

RBAC and admission policies are the second lock, in the API server itself. They are there for the day the portal has a bug, or worse.

The record

A shell nobody watched live is still a shell everybody can replay. That is the trade that makes the rest acceptable.
