Break-glass · 02 of 07 · The shape

How a session works

Tower never hands out access. Its backend borrows a narrow role for itself, in one namespace, for one session, and gives it back when the session ends.

No credential to leak

The browser only ever talks to Backstage. There is no kubeconfig to copy, no token to paste into a laptop, nothing to revoke afterwards.

Borrowed, briefly

Backstage can create RoleBindings to two debug roles and nothing else. Each session binds one, in one namespace, and deletes it at the end.

Evidence elsewhere

Recordings land in a locked bucket on the dev cluster, not on the prod cluster they record, and the hash goes into a transparency log.
