Three layers, three ideas of "ready"

Crossplane owns Ready and Synced. Each composed resource reports health its own way, or not at all. Getting an honest answer to "is my database ready?" meant building a condition of my own on top.

Don't call it Ready

function-go-templating reserves Ready, Healthy and Synced and errors if you set them. My own condition is ComponentReady, with a closed list of reasons such as PostgreSQLDegraded or RedisReleaseFailed, so it never races Crossplane's.

Mark what can't report

A Service, a PVC or a ConfigMap has no status conditions, so auto-ready waits for them forever. The Dex component sat at "Creating" with its pod running until the function marked those resources ready itself.

Provider bugs look like readiness bugs

A RepositoryFile interrupted mid-create stayed Ready: False forever. The fix was in the provider, not the composition: a deterministic external name, patched in my fork.
