SLOs · 02 of 04 · Three signals

Three signals, three questions

A single "is it red?" hides three different questions: is the budget spent, is it being spent right now, and can we see the SLO at all. Each is its own signal with its own threshold, its own clear level and its own notification.

Budget exhausted

Whole-window burn rate above 1× means the window's error budget is spent. It lags: an outage that ended an hour ago can leave it red for days. It clears below 0.9×.

Burning fast

Both windows above 14.4× is 2% of a 30-day budget gone in an hour. Both must agree to fire, so a one-minute spike is ignored; either dropping below 13× clears it.

No data

A failed Prometheus query does not count as a miss, so an outage never reads as every SLO going blind. New SLOs get about 30 minutes while Sloth loads their rules.
