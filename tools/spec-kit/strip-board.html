How this board works
  1. Everything goes on a strip. A strip has an id that never changes and is never reused: A ask, Y yours, I issue, W work, C change, R unverified, O one you added.
  2. Chat stays short. A chat message names strip ids and says only what changed: what needs you, what broke, what went live. The detail is here.
  3. A question is never asked only in chat. Each ask carries its options, Claude's recommendation, what it blocks, and what Claude does if you say nothing. Things that cannot be undone, or that only you may do, never have a default.
  4. Answer here or in chat. Pick an option and press Answer, or type A-7: b in chat. A chat answer is copied onto the strip word for word.
  5. Claude reads the board at the start of every turn and before acting on anything an ask gates. Your notes and new strips are picked up then.
  6. A change says where it is live and how that was checked. Anything claimed but not proven gets its own R strip until it is.
  7. Strips are closed, never deleted. Answered asks stay under Decided as the record of what was decided, when, and in whose words. Fixed issues and finished work move to Closed, so Issues and In flight show only what is still outstanding.
